# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a calm, useful place for everyone — regardless of experience level, language,
nationality, gender, religion or anything else that has nothing to do with the quality of a contribution.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intentions.
- Critique the code, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic; move unrelated debates elsewhere.
- Be patient with people writing in their second (or third) language.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, harassment or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Spam, or issues/PRs that exist only to advertise something.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space associated with the repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, and may block accounts that ignore these rules.
Report a problem by opening a private security advisory or contacting the maintainer through their GitHub
profile. Reports are handled discreetly.
